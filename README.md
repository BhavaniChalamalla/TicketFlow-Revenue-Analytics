# TicketFlow Revenue Analytics
Power BI dashboard analyzing ticket sales trends, operational performance, customer activity, and revenue insights for a trackless train business.

## Overview
TicketFlow Revenue Analytics is a business intelligence and revenue analytics project developed using Power BI. The dashboard analyzes ticket sales, customer activity, operational performance, and revenue trends for a trackless train entertainment operation.

The project focuses on transforming raw transactional and operational data into interactive visualizations and actionable business insights to support data-driven decision-making.

---

## Objectives
- Analyze ticket sales and revenue performance
- Identify peak demand periods and seasonal trends
- Monitor operational efficiency and ride activity
- Evaluate customer purchasing and payment behavior
- Develop KPI-driven business intelligence reporting

---

## Dashboard Features
- Revenue and ticket sales trend analysis
- Daily, weekly, and seasonal performance tracking
- Peak operational period identification
- Cash vs Card payment analysis
- KPI monitoring and performance metrics
- Interactive filtering and drill-down capabilities
- Business-focused visual storytelling
